01

Before you commit

Land, budget and council — the decisions that set your ceiling before a builder is involved.

  • Walking into a builder’s showroom without knowing what your home should cost puts you at an immediate disadvantage. You have no reference point, and you cannot assess whether the quote is competitive, realistic or inflated.

    This service gives you an independent preliminary cost assessment — based on current Melbourne market conditions, your specific design and your site.

    What we provide

    • Preliminary construction cost range for your proposed home
    • Site cost assessment based on land conditions
    • Professional consultant fees guide — architect, engineer, surveyor, energy assessor
    • Council and statutory fee estimates
    • Provisional sum and PC item guidance
    • Contingency allowance recommendation
    • Key cost risks identified for your specific project

China Procurement Assistance

Access quality building materials through established supply channels

Melbourne’s construction industry has long sourced materials, fixtures and fittings through Chinese manufacturing. The challenge is knowing which suppliers are reliable, which products meet Australian standards, and how to manage quality, shipping, customs and compliance without getting burnt.

Talk to us about sourcing

What we assist with

  • Identifying suitable product categories for overseas procurement
  • Supplier assessment guidance
  • Australian Standards compliance considerations
  • Practical advice on quality verification before shipment
  • Freight, customs and duty considerations
  • Risk assessment — what to procure offshore and what to source locally
