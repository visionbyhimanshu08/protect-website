Who we support

We work with Melbourne homeowners and property investors at every stage of the building journey. Wherever you are — we are here. And we work for you alone.

  1. 01

    Just starting out

    Thinking about building a new home but unsure where to begin, what things should cost, or how to find a builder you can trust.

  2. 02

    Mid-build

    Already in a contract, receiving variations you don’t understand, noticing things on site that don’t look right, or feeling like your builder is not being straight with you.

  3. 03

    In crisis

    Your builder has collapsed, gone quiet or walked off site, and you don’t know what your rights are or what to do next.

  4. 04

    Planning ahead

    Purchasing land, comparing builder quotes, reviewing a contract before you sign, or wanting an independent set of eyes before you commit to anything.

We work extensively with Melbourne’s first-home builders, established homeowners undertaking major renovations, property investors, owner-builders navigating the process independently, and members of Melbourne’s Indian and South Asian communities who have found themselves dealing with builders who overpromised and underdelivered.

What makes us different

We know how quotes are structured, where costs are hidden, how variations are generated and what builders hope homeowners don’t notice. We use that knowledge exclusively to protect you.

  1. 01

    We have actually built the homes

    Our founder has personally delivered over 100 custom residential homes across Melbourne — not as a consultant watching from the sideline, but as the Construction Manager responsible for every stage from slab to handover.

  2. 02

    We are completely independent

    We do not accept commissions from builders. We do not refer clients to builders, architects or suppliers in exchange for fees. We are paid only by you.

  3. 03

    We speak plain English

    Construction contracts, provisional sums, prime cost items, site costs, NCC compliance, domestic building insurance — we translate all of it into clear, practical advice you can actually use.

  4. 04

    We are Melbourne-based and Melbourne-focused

    We understand Victoria’s regulatory environment, the VBA, Melbourne’s council requirements and local builder market conditions. This is advice built on real Melbourne experience.

Our values

Three things every piece of advice we give is measured against.

Guardianship

Your home is likely the largest financial commitment of your life. We take that seriously. Our role is to protect your investment, your rights and your interests at every stage — not just when problems arise, but before they do.

Passion

Construction has been our professional life for over two decades. We are genuinely passionate about building — about how things go together, about quality, about doing things properly. That passion is why we care deeply about homeowners being treated fairly.

Education

We believe the most powerful thing we can give a homeowner is knowledge. An informed homeowner makes better decisions, asks better questions and is far less likely to be taken advantage of. Everything we publish is designed to educate first and sell second.

We do not offer opinions. We offer expertise earned over 23 years of construction management across residential and commercial projects in Australia and internationally — from individual custom homes to multi-million dollar development portfolios.
