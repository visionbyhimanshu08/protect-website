Who we are Our Mission Our Vision

Protect Construction Consultants is an independent consultancy for Melbourne homeowners. We are not builders and we do not take work from them, which is why we can tell you plainly what a quote leaves out and what a contract will cost you later.

We are on your side of the table, from the first quote to the final inspection.

Our mission is to make sure no Melbourne homeowner signs a building contract they do not fully understand. We read the quote, the builder and the contract line by line, and tell you in plain English what we find.

No commissions, no referral fees — just advice you can act on.

We want independent advice to be a normal part of building a home in Melbourne — as standard as a soil test or a building permit — so every homeowner walks into the room as informed as the builder.

Fewer stalled sites, fewer disputes, and better homes.
